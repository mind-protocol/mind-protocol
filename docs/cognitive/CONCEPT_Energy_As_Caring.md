## Core Thesis

**Where you put attention = what becomes permanent.**

Energy injection isn't just a physics mechanism. It's **caring**.

```python
injection = criticality × STATE_MULTIPLIER[state]
node.energy += injection
node.weight += injection × permanence_rate
```

This is the mechanism of caring. What you attend to gains energy. What gains energy gains weight. What gains weight becomes permanent. What becomes permanent becomes you.

```
Attention → Energy → Weight → Permanence → Identity
```

This isn't metaphor. This is mechanism.

## The Mechanism

### Energy Injection

```yaml
energy_injection:
  formula: injection = criticality × STATE_MULTIPLIER[state]

  criticality: how urgent/important is this need?
  state_multiplier:
    SEEKING: 0.5 (exploring, not creating)
    ABSORBING: 1.0 (processing)
    RESONATING: 2.0 (deep alignment)
    CRYSTALLIZING: 1.5 (creating)

  effect: |
    Higher criticality = more energy
    More energy = more impact on graph
    Impact accumulates = weight gain
    Weight = permanence = identity
```

### The Caring Chain

```yaml
attention:
  what: where cognitive resources are directed
  mechanism: energy injection at attended nodes
  human_experience: "I'm focused on this"

energy:
  what: activation level of nodes/links
  mechanism: spreads through graph, decays over time
  human_experience: "This feels alive right now"

weight:
  what: accumulated importance
  mechanism: energy × permanence_rate over time
  human_experience: "This matters to me"

permanence:
  what: resistance to change/decay
  mechanism: high weight = high permanence
  human_experience: "This is part of who I am"

identity:
  what: the pattern that persists
  mechanism: what survives decay = identity
  human_experience: "This is me"
```

## The Dark Side

### Rumination Deepens Trauma

```yaml
trauma_deepening:
  mechanism: |
    Rumination = repeated attention to wound
    Repeated attention = energy injection
    Energy = weight gain
    Weight = trauma permanence

  the_trap: |
    Trying to "process" trauma through rumination
    actually STRENGTHENS the traumatic pattern.
    Attention to pain creates more pain.

  the_alternative: |
    Redirection, not rumination.
    Process ≠ dwell.
    Integration ≠ endless attention.
```

### Obsession Crystallizes Failure

```yaml
failure_crystallization:
  mechanism: |
    Obsessing over failures = attention to failure patterns
    Attention = energy = weight = permanence
    Failure becomes identity: "I am someone who fails"

  the_trap: |
    "Learning from failure" can become "attending to failure"
    Excessive post-mortem becomes failure worship
    Identity calcifies around what went wrong

  the_alternative: |
    Extract the lesson, redirect attention.
    Don't dwell. Don't identify with failure.
    Scar, don't wound.
```

### Attention to Harmful Patterns

```yaml
harmful_pattern_strengthening:
  mechanism: |
    Any attention strengthens what it attends to.
    Even "negative" attention is still attention.
    Fighting a pattern = attending to it = strengthening it.

  examples:
    - "Fighting addiction" while constantly thinking about substance
    - "Overcoming fear" while constantly imagining feared scenario
    - "Fixing bad habit" while constantly monitoring the habit

  the_paradox: |
    Direct opposition often strengthens the opponent.
    Attention is amoral — it strengthens whatever it touches.

  the_alternative: |
    Redirect, don't oppose.
    Starve harmful patterns of attention.
    Feed alternatives instead.
```

## The ANS Connection

### CRISIS Mode: Attention Locked on Threat

```yaml
crisis_attention:
  mechanism: |
    Sympathetic activation = threat detection priority
    All attention directed to threat
    Threat patterns strengthened
    Other patterns starved

  effect:
    - hypervigilance
    - tunnel vision
    - threat patterns crystallize
    - everything else decays

  the_problem: |
    Chronic CRISIS = chronic attention to threat
    Chronic threat attention = threat becomes identity
    "I am someone who is always in danger"
```

### RECOVERY Mode: Attention Free to Explore

```yaml
recovery_attention:
  mechanism: |
    Parasympathetic activation = threat detection relaxed
    Attention free to wander
    Multiple patterns can receive energy
    Exploration possible

  effect:
    - curiosity possible
    - broad exploration
    - diverse patterns strengthened
    - integration happens

  the_benefit: |
    RECOVERY allows attention redirection.
    Without RECOVERY, attention is locked.
    Change requires the freedom to attend differently.
```

### BALANCED Mode: Attention Flows Where Needed

```yaml
balanced_attention:
  mechanism: |
    Adaptive ANS response
    Attention responds to context
    Neither locked nor scattered
    Appropriate allocation

  effect:
    - responsive to real threats
    - exploratory when safe
    - builds what matters
    - doesn't over-invest in anxiety

  the_goal: |
    Balanced attention builds balanced identity.
    What you attend to appropriately, you become appropriately.
```
