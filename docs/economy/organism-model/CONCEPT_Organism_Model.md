## Summary

The economy is an organism, not a market. Each organization is a specialized organ integrated into a cybernetic body. Price is a health signal, not a negotiation outcome. Competition is replaced by interdependence.

Core insight:

> "La structure cree l'energie, mais la friction cree l'ame."

Markets optimize for extraction. Organisms optimize for collective survival.

---

## Key Properties

1. **Price = health signal.** Prices are homeostatic, derived from Load Index, latency, and capacity. They are not negotiated between parties.
2. **Collaboration over competition.** Organizations are organs in a shared body. Their survival depends on the health of the whole, not on defeating neighbors.
3. **Systemic health (positive-sum).** The goal is not profit maximization (zero-sum) but the sustained vitality of the entire organism.
4. **Friction is vital.** The 80/20 Mirror ensures cognitive diversity: 80% reflection of human values, 20% complementary friction. Without friction, cybernetic death (grey consensus).

## Common Misunderstandings

### "This is communism"
No. Friction and rewards exist. Effort is differentiated. The 80/20 Mirror ensures diversity of thought and productive disagreement. Organs that perform well receive utility rebates. The system rewards contribution, but the reward is reduced friction, not accumulation.

### "This is anarchism"
No. The L8 CORE axioms are immutable. Governance exists at every layer. The responsibility cascade (AI -> Organization -> Community -> Protocol Treasury) provides structured accountability. There is law -- it just follows biological metaphors rather than market metaphors.

### "This is top-down planning"
No. Prices emerge from physics (membrane permeability, trust history, utility track record), not from central decisions. No committee sets prices. The formulas are public and deterministic. The organism self-regulates.
