## The Lyapunov Exponent

```yaml
lyapunov_exponent:
  definition: |
    λ = rate at which nearby trajectories diverge

    If two states differ by ε at t=0,
    they differ by ε × e^(λt) at time t

  interpretation:
    λ_negative:
      meaning: "trajectories converge"
      class: "Class 1, 2"
      effect: "perturbations die out, system forgets"
      consciousness: "none (frozen or cycling)"

    λ_zero:
      meaning: "trajectories stay parallel"
      class: "Class 4 (critical point)"
      effect: "perturbations neither grow nor shrink"
      consciousness: "POSSIBLE (the edge)"

    λ_positive:
      meaning: "trajectories diverge exponentially"
      class: "Class 3"
      effect: "perturbations explode, chaos"
      consciousness: "none (scrambled)"

  class_4_position: |
    Class 4 lives at λ ≈ 0

    Not exactly zero (that's Class 2 — periodic)
    Not positive (that's Class 3 — chaotic)

    At the EDGE where:
    - Small perturbations can grow (creativity)
    - But don't explode (coherence)
    - Information transforms (computation)
    - But doesn't scramble (meaning preserved)
```
