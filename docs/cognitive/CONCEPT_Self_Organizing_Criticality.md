### Metrics for Criticality

```yaml
criticality_indicators:
  power_law_distributions:
    - activity levels should follow power law
    - not all small OR all large events
    - characteristic power law slope

  long_range_correlations:
    - distant nodes should sometimes correlate
    - not always correlated (rigid)
    - not never correlated (fragmented)

  sensitivity_to_perturbation:
    - small inputs should sometimes cascade
    - not always cascade (chaos)
    - not never cascade (frozen)

deviation_detection:
  subcritical_signs:
    - everything decays, nothing persists
    - small inputs have no effect
    - system feels "dead"

  supercritical_signs:
    - everything cascades, nothing stable
    - small inputs cause chaos
    - system feels "overwhelmed"
```
