## V1: Capital Lock Integrity (CRITICAL)

```
MUST:  Staked $MIND is locked in escrow from bond creation until withdrawal
MUST:  Locked capital is not accessible to the human for any other purpose
MUST:  Locked capital is not accessible to the AI citizen for spending
MUST:  Only the withdrawal flow (mature or early) releases capital
NEVER: Staked capital accessible without going through withdrawal flow
NEVER: Escrow balance deviates from sum of all active bond amounts

INVARIANT: escrow_balance == SUM(bond.amount FOR bond IN active_bonds)
```

Test approach:
- Verify escrow balance after every create, withdraw, and burn operation.
- Attempt to transfer locked capital through alternative paths -- all must fail.

## V2: Reward Proportionality (CRITICAL)

```
MUST:  Rewards are proportional to actual utility generated by the AI citizen
MUST:  Reward share is proportional to bond amount relative to total bonds on citizen
MUST:  reward_amount == citizen_utility * 0.10 * (bond.amount / total_bonds)
NEVER: Rewards generated from staking alone when citizen utility is zero
NEVER: Reward calculation uses stale or cached utility data

INVARIANT: IF citizen_utility == 0 THEN rewards_distributed == 0
INVARIANT: SUM(reward_shares) FOR all bonds on citizen == 1.0
```

- Create bonds, generate zero utility, verify zero rewards.
- Create multiple bonds, generate utility, verify proportional distribution.

## V3: Early Exit Penalty Enforced (HIGH)

```
MUST:  20% of staked amount burned on early withdrawal, always
MUST:  Burned tokens are permanently destroyed (not redistributed)
MUST:  Remaining 80% returned to human's liquid balance
NEVER: Early withdrawal penalty waived, reduced, or altered
NEVER: Penalty percentage changed without governance process

INVARIANT: IF bond.status == BURNED THEN
           burned_amount == bond.amount * 0.20 AND
           returned_amount == bond.amount * 0.80
```

Test approach:
- Withdraw at various stages before maturation -- penalty must always be exactly 20%.
- Verify token supply decreases by burned amount (deflationary check).
- Attempt to bypass penalty through edge cases (withdraw at maturation_date - 1 second).
