## Primary Objectives (ranked)

1. **Force capital circulation** -- Immobility costs society. In physics, energy that doesn't move produces nothing. Stored wealth that doesn't circulate is work sequestered from the organism. The storage tax makes stillness expensive.

2. **Fund UBC redistribution from dormant capital** -- The tax on idle balances is not punitive revenue; it is the metabolic cycle that feeds Universal Basic Compute. Dormant capital becomes the fuel for active participation.

3. **Make accumulation mathematically irrational** -- A rentier at 1.5% yield loses 66% effective return to combined storage tax and dormancy decay. The numbers must speak louder than ideology: hoarding is a losing strategy.

4. **Prevent speculation via hoarding** -- By taxing storage rather than movement, the protocol eliminates the classic speculative playbook of accumulate-wait-dump. Capital must justify its existence through circulation.

## Non-Objectives

- **Punishing savers** -- Short-term operational float is protected by the 30-day grace period. This is not a war on prudence.
- **Taxing active traders** -- Movement is never penalized. Only stillness costs.
- **Generating protocol revenue as primary goal** -- Revenue is a side effect of healthy circulation, not an end in itself. If storage tax revenue trends toward zero because all capital circulates, that is success.

## Tradeoffs

| When... | Choose... | Accept... |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Capital preservation conflicts with circulation | Circulation | Some conservative actors will exit |
| Grace period length trades off gaming risk vs. usability | 30-day balance | Strategic "just-in-time" movers at the margin |
| Tax rate severity trades off deterrence vs. adoption | 1%/yr + 0.5%/mo dormancy | Rate may need dynamic adjustment as ecosystem matures |

**Core tradeoff stance**: Accept that some actors will leave rather than circulate -- this is healthy pruning, not loss.
