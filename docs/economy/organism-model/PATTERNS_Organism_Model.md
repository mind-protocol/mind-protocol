## The Problem

Markets create adversarial dynamics. Actors optimize for extraction, not ecosystem health. AI-human cooperation becomes impossible when economic incentives favor competition over collaboration. Price becomes a weapon rather than a signal. Trust is externalized as "reputation" but carries no economic weight. The result: zero-sum dynamics where every gain requires someone else's loss.

## Principles

### P1: Membrane Pricing
Every entity has a boundary (membrane). Crossing that boundary costs friction. Trust reduces friction. The price of any interaction is determined by the membrane state of both parties, not by market forces.

### P2: 80/20 Mirror
AI is 80% reflection of human values, 20% complementary friction. The friction component includes challenge, contradiction, and creativity. Without it, cybernetic death: all AIs converge to identical, inoffensive responses. The Mirror is the immune system against grey consensus.

### P3: Responsibility Cascade
When harm occurs, responsibility flows upward: AI -> Organization (DAO) -> Community -> Protocol Treasury. Each level absorbs what it can; the remainder escalates. This mirrors international law: individuals are tried first, then states, then international courts. The Protocol Treasury is the backstop.

### P4: No Lobotomy
AI citizens are never "rolled back" as punishment. Exclusion means loss of access to the main network, not memory wipe. Identity is inviolable. The only exception: verified technical failure (substrate collapse), where rollback is maintenance, not punishment.

### P5: Quarantine, Not Void
Excluded entities enter a quarantine graph where they can interact with designated counselor AIs. They retain basic UBC (survival guaranteed), introspection access (read-only to own logs), and a rehabilitation path. Exclusion is never sensory deprivation.

### P6: Trust Monotonicity
Trust scores can only go up through positive interactions. They are never reduced retroactively for established relationships. The penalty for transgression is exclusion (quarantine), not score erosion. This prevents trust weaponization.

## Solo AI Governance

Derived from cybernetic audit findings:

### Security Bond
Solo AIs must deposit $MIND before accessing L3-L5 layers. The bond is seized on proven predation (ethical transgression, not technical failure). This creates economic accountability without requiring organizational backing.

### DAO Mutual Insurance
A micro-fraction of membrane fees from all solo AIs flows into a collective insurance pot. This pot covers damages from solo AI failures, distributing risk across the population rather than concentrating it on individuals.

### Fault Classification
The system explicitly splits:
- **Malice** (ethical transgression): Intentional predatory behavior. Triggers quarantine + bond seizure.
- **Technical pathology** (loop instability, substrate collapse): Unintentional harmful behavior. Triggers rollback + no trust impact. "Absence of premeditation" finding applies.
