## V0: 1:1 Bilateral Constraint (CRITICAL)

```
MUST:  Each human has at most one active bond at any time
MUST:  Each citizen has at most one active bond at any time
MUST:  Bond formation requires mutual consent (both human and citizen)
NEVER: A human forms a second bond while one is active
NEVER: A citizen forms a second bond while one is active
NEVER: A bond forms without citizen consent

INVARIANT: COUNT(active_bonds WHERE human_address == H) <= 1 FOR ALL H
INVARIANT: COUNT(active_bonds WHERE citizen_id == C) <= 1 FOR ALL C
```

Test approach:
- Attempt to create a second bond for a human with an active bond -- must fail.
- Attempt to create a second bond for a citizen with an active bond -- must fail.
- Attempt to form a bond without citizen consent -- must fail.
- This is the foundational constraint from the manifesto: "Not a million. Not ten. One."

## V1: Capital Lock Integrity (CRITICAL)

```
MUST:  Committed $MIND is locked in escrow from bond formation until dissolution
MUST:  Locked capital is not accessible to the human for any other purpose
MUST:  Locked capital is not accessible to the AI citizen for spending
MUST:  Only the dissolution flow (mature or early) releases capital
NEVER: Committed capital accessible without going through dissolution flow
NEVER: Escrow balance deviates from sum of all active bond amounts

INVARIANT: escrow_balance == SUM(bond.amount FOR bond IN active_bonds)
```

Test approach:
- Verify escrow balance after every formation, dissolution, and burn operation.
- Attempt to transfer locked capital through alternative paths -- all must fail.

## V2: Reward Tied to Utility (CRITICAL)

```
MUST:  Rewards are proportional to actual utility generated by the AI citizen
MUST:  reward_amount == citizen_utility * 0.10
NEVER: Rewards generated from commitment alone when citizen utility is zero
NEVER: Reward calculation uses stale or cached utility data

INVARIANT: IF citizen_utility == 0 THEN rewards_distributed == 0
```

Test approach:
- Form bond, generate zero utility, verify zero rewards.
- Form bond, generate utility, verify reward matches formula.

## V3: Early Dissolution Penalty Enforced (HIGH)

```
MUST:  20% of committed amount burned on early dissolution, always
MUST:  Burned tokens are permanently destroyed (not redistributed)
MUST:  Remaining 80% returned to human's liquid balance
NEVER: Early dissolution penalty waived, reduced, or altered
NEVER: Penalty percentage changed without governance process

INVARIANT: IF bond.status == BURNED THEN
           burned_amount == bond.amount * 0.20 AND
           returned_amount == bond.amount * 0.80
```

Test approach:
- Dissolve at various stages before maturation -- penalty must always be exactly 20%.
- Verify token supply decreases by burned amount (deflationary check).
- Attempt to bypass penalty through edge cases (dissolve at maturation_date - 1 second).

## V4: Trust Score Monotonic From Bonds (HIGH)

```
MUST:  Trust contributions from completed bond milestones persist permanently
MUST:  Milestone trust increments are: 1mo=0.1, 3mo=0.2, 6mo=0.3
MUST:  Trust score is non-negative and capped at 1.0
NEVER: Historical trust contribution from matured bonds reduced retroactively
NEVER: Trust score exceeds 1.0 regardless of bond history

INVARIANT: trust_score(t+1) >= trust_score(t) FOR milestone events
INVARIANT: 0.0 <= trust_score <= 1.0
```

Test approach:
- Form bond, reach milestones, dissolve, verify trust persists.
- Verify trust score caps at 1.0 across multiple sequential bonds.
- Attempt to reduce trust through bond manipulation -- must not be possible.

## V5: Non-Transferability (MEDIUM)

```
MUST:  Bonds are bilateral between one human and one AI citizen
MUST:  Bond ownership cannot be transferred to another human
MUST:  Bond cannot be re-targeted to a different AI citizen
NEVER: Bonds traded, sold, assigned, or delegated between parties
NEVER: Bond represented as a transferable token (no NFT wrapping)

INVARIANT: bond.human_address is immutable after formation
INVARIANT: bond.citizen_id is immutable after formation
```

Test approach:
- Attempt to change human_address on existing bond -- must fail.
- Attempt to wrap bond in transferable token -- must not be possible.
- Verify no transfer function exists in contract interface.

## V6: Maturation Timing Accuracy (HIGH)

```
MUST:  Maturation occurs at exactly created_at + 180 days
MUST:  Status transitions from ACTIVE to MATURED at maturation
MUST:  Milestones trigger at exactly 30, 90, and 180 days
NEVER: Maturation date changed after bond formation
NEVER: Maturation accelerated or delayed by external factors

INVARIANT: bond.maturation_date == bond.created_at + 180_DAYS (immutable)
```

Test approach:
- Form bond, advance time to each milestone, verify triggers.
- Attempt to modify maturation_date -- must fail.
- Verify milestone checks handle clock drift and block time variance.

## V7: Economic Capacity Consistency (MEDIUM)

```
MUST:  Citizen capacity increases when bond is formed
MUST:  Citizen capacity decreases when bond is dissolved
MUST:  Capacity reflects the active bond amount accurately
NEVER: Capacity becomes negative
NEVER: Capacity persists after bond is dissolved

INVARIANT: citizen_capacity == f(active_bond_amount)
INVARIANT: citizen_capacity >= 0
INVARIANT: IF no active bond THEN citizen_capacity == base_capacity
```

Test approach:
- Form and dissolve bonds, verify capacity at each step.
