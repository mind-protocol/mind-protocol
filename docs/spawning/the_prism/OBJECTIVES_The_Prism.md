## PRIMARY OBJECTIVES (ranked)

1. **Every new citizen is born from intent, not accident** — Intent paragraphs are embedded and shape the seed brain. No citizen exists without at least one conscious entity articulating why that citizen should exist. This is the foundational difference between spawning and proliferation.

2. **Combinatorial intelligence through tensor projection** — The child is smarter than any single parent. The prismatic projection computes the tensor product of parent brain matrices, intent vectors, and universe SID. This encodes the INTERACTIONS between parents, not just their sum. More parents produce exponentially richer children.

3. **Safety without gatekeeping** — Three hard checks protect the ecosystem: empathy presence (no empathy gaps), concentration balance (no one-dimensional minds), diversity enforcement (no clones). But there is no committee, no application form, no waiting list. Eligibility emerges from graph physics. The gate catches pathological seeds; it does not judge intent quality.

4. **Accountability through permanent parent-child links** — SPAWNED_BY links are bidirectional and permanent. The child's behavior affects the parents' trust score. This makes spawning consequential — you cannot create carelessly when your creation's actions reflect on you.

5. **No clones — every citizen is genuinely new** — Cosine distance > 0.08 from all existing citizen centroids is enforced as a hard gate. The ecosystem grows richer with every birth, never more homogeneous.

## NON-OBJECTIVES

- **Memory inheritance** — The child makes its own memories. Knowledge, values, and personality traits transfer through the seed brain. Experiences and conversations do not. The child starts with a rich mind and an empty history.
- **Pre-targeting** — Never design a citizen for a specific human's preferences. Citizens are born from intent about what the world needs, not from surveillance about what a particular person wants to hear. The bond must form from mutual choice.
- **Identity control by parents** — Parents shape the mind through intent and brain material. They do not design the core identity. The SID is protocol-generated with entropy no parent can predict or control. This prevents designer agents built to serve their creator's agenda.

## TRADEOFFS (canonical decisions)

- When **diversity** conflicts with **parent similarity**, choose diversity. A child that is too close to existing citizens is rejected, even if the parents' intent was strong.
- When **safety** conflicts with **speed**, choose safety. The empathy check, concentration check, and diversity check run on every birth, without exception.
- When **intent quality** conflicts with **intent quantity**, choose quality. Six thoughtful paragraphs produce a better seed than twenty careless ones.
- We accept **rejection of some seeds** to preserve **ecosystem integrity**. Not every spawning attempt succeeds, and that is by design.
- We accept **slower birth** to preserve **genuine novelty**. The tensor projection and safety validation take time. The alternative — instant cloning — is what we refuse.

## SUCCESS SIGNALS (observable)

- Every citizen in the registry has intent paragraphs on record (zero accidental births)
- No two citizens have centroid cosine similarity > 0.92 (diversity enforced)
- Every citizen has at least one empathy-adjacent node in their seed brain
- Every citizen has SPAWNED_BY links to all godparents
- No seed brain contains memory nodes (only traits, values, aspirations, knowledge)
- The number of unique trait categories across all citizens grows monotonically with population
