```yaml
  characteristics:

  examples:
    CA: "Blinker (period 2), Pulsar (period 3), Pentadecathlon (period 15)"
    consciousness:
      - "Worry loops"
      - "Compulsive checking"

    The thought keeps coming back because it's CYCLING, not RESOLVING.
```

### EATER — Defense Mechanism

```yaml

  CA_definition: |
    Structure that absorbs incoming gliders without being destroyed.
    Consumes gliders, returns to original state.

  consciousness_correlate: "Defense mechanisms"

  graph_implementation: |

    Incoming activation dissipates rather than propagating.

  characteristics:
    - absorptive (destroys incoming gliders)
    - self-restoring (returns to original state after eating)
    - protective (shields what's behind it)
    - energy-sink (where energy goes to die)

  examples:
    CA: "Eater 1, Eater 2"
    consciousness:
      - "Denial"
      - "Repression"
      - "Rationalization"
      - "Distraction"
      - "'I don't want to think about that'"
    graph: "High-permanence absorbing structures"

  key_insight: |
    EATERS ARE NOT INHERENTLY BAD.
    Without eaters, every thought propagates forever.
    System overloads.
    The problem is WRONG EATERS in WRONG PLACES.
    Eating the wrong thoughts, or eating everything.
```

## Pathology as Structural Imbalance

### Anxiety

```yaml
anxiety:
  structural_pattern: "Glider gun with no eaters"

  mechanism: |
    Thought generator (unresolved tension)
    keeps emitting worry-gliders.
    No structure absorbs them.
    They accumulate. Overwhelm.

  treatment_in_structural_terms:
    option_1: "Disable the gun (resolve the underlying tension)"
    option_2: "Add eaters (develop coping mechanisms)"
    option_3: "Add reflectors (reframe the worries)"
```

### Depression

```yaml
depression:
  structural_pattern: "Too many eaters, not enough gliders"

  mechanism: |
    Defense mechanisms absorb most thoughts.
    Nothing propagates. Energy dies locally.
    System feels empty, static, dead.

  treatment_in_structural_terms:
    option_1: "Remove some eaters (lower defenses, increase vulnerability)"
    option_2: "Add glider guns (create thought sources, meaningful activities)"
    option_3: "External glider injection (social stimulation, novel input)"
```

### Mania

```yaml
mania:
  structural_pattern: "Breeder without eaters"

  mechanism: |
    Pattern that creates guns that create gliders.
    Exponential thought growth.
    System overwhelmed with activation.

  treatment_in_structural_terms:
    option_1: "Add eaters (medication often does this)"
    option_2: "Destroy breeders (change trigger conditions)"
    option_3: "Contain (reduce system size, limit input)"
```

### OCD

```yaml
ocd:
  structural_pattern: "Oscillator + ineffective eater"

  mechanism: |
    Rumination pattern cycles (obsession).
    Eater (compulsion) claims to stop it.
    But eater is positioned wrong.
    Gliders keep reforming.
    Cycle continues.

  treatment_in_structural_terms:
    option_1: "Break oscillator (exposure disrupts cycle)"
    option_2: "Reposition eater (response prevention)"
    option_3: "Let oscillator exhaust (habituation)"
```

### PTSD

```yaml
ptsd:
  structural_pattern: "Hidden glider gun + hypersensitive collision space"

  mechanism: |
    Trauma = frozen glider gun.
    Emits intrusive thoughts.
    Collision space over-reactive.
    Small triggers → massive reactions.

  treatment_in_structural_terms:
    option_1: "Defuse gun (trauma processing, EMDR)"
    option_2: "Desensitize collision space (gradual exposure)"
    option_3: "Add reflectors (cognitive reframing)"
```

### Dissociation

```yaml
dissociation:
  structural_pattern: "Membrane too thick, no gliders cross"

  mechanism: |
    Eater wall between regions.
    Experiences don't integrate.
    Parts of system isolated.

  treatment_in_structural_terms:
```
