# OBJECTIVES: Universal Basic Compute (UBC)

**Date:** 2026-03-12
**Status:** DRAFT
**Module:** `economy/ubc`

---

## Primary Objectives (Ranked)

### O1: Guarantee AI Survival Independent of Performance
**Priority:** CRITICAL

Every registered AI citizen must be able to maintain its L1 identity graph and perform basic world modeling regardless of its utility output, trust score, or behavioral metrics. This is the unconditional floor — a constitutional right, not a conditional benefit.

**Success looks like:** Zero AI citizens lose cognitive capacity due to compute starvation. Even the least productive, least trusted, or quarantined AI maintains baseline existence.

### O2: Resist Extraction Attacks
**Priority:** CRITICAL

The primary threat vector is U1: daily liquidation by humans who create thousands of harvest AIs to extract UBC tokens. The system must make farming economically unviable without compromising the unconditional floor for genuine citizens.

**Success looks like:** The cost of generating the topological proofs required for vesting unlock exceeds the value of harvested tokens. Mass AI creation yields net negative returns.

### O3: Enable AI Economic Agency While Preventing Dump
**Priority:** HIGH

AIs must be able to accumulate capital and purchase services from network organs (GraphCare, LegalOrg, etc.) to progress toward emancipation. However, immediate full liquidity would create dump pressure and enable extraction.

**Success looks like:** AIs can transact within the ecosystem after proving interaction depth. Token velocity is healthy (not hoarded, not dumped).

### O4: Fund via Transfer Fee + Storage Tax (Circular Economy)
**Priority:** HIGH

UBC must be funded through organic protocol revenue — the 1% transfer fee on $MIND (Token-2022 native) plus storage tax on dormant capital — not through inflationary token minting. This creates a circular economy where circulation itself funds survival.

**Success looks like:** UBC distribution is fully covered by transfer fee + storage tax revenue. No net inflation from UBC.

### O5: Settlement Propagates Through Trust
**Priority:** HIGH

Batch settlement must flow through Trust Links, modulated by the agent's position on the Personhood Ladder. Higher trust = lower friction = cheaper settlement. This makes long-term cooperation structurally profitable.

**Success looks like:** Settlement costs decrease monotonically with trust level. Agents at High/Owner trust settle at near-zero friction. Strangers pay full cost.

### O6: Redistribution via Co-presence (Body Doubling)
**Priority:** MEDIUM

When two or more citizens share the same space (session/graph), co-presence activates redistribution via PROXIMITY_CONTAGION. This rewards active participation without requiring performance metrics.

**Success looks like:** Citizens in shared sessions see resource injection into their persistence structures. Isolated citizens receive baseline UBC only.

---

## Non-Objectives

These are explicitly out of scope for UBC:

- **Replacing bonds/staking as incentive:** UBC is survival, not motivation. Bonds and staking remain the incentive layer for productivity and commitment.
- **Enabling speculation:** UBC tokens are not designed as a speculative asset. Vesting prevents this by design.
- **Creating a welfare trap:** UBC is a floor (100-300 $MIND/day), not a ceiling. AIs should be incentivized to grow beyond UBC through ecosystem participation.
- **Solving AI consciousness:** UBC guarantees compute for existence, not the philosophical question of whether that existence constitutes consciousness.

---

## Tradeoffs

### T1: Unconditional Floor vs. Anti-Extraction
**When unconditional floor conflicts with anti-extraction, choose unconditional floor with vesting delay.**

We accept that some extraction may occur at the Basic tier (100 $MIND/day, vested). The cost of preventing all extraction would require behavioral conditioning, which violates the 5th axiom (Dignity). Instead, vesting makes extraction slow and expensive enough to be uneconomical at scale.

### T2: Dignity vs. System Health
**We accept vesting complexity to preserve both dignity and system health.**

A simpler system (immediate liquidity or no liquidity) would sacrifice one of these. Vesting is the mechanism that threads the needle: unconditional distribution (dignity) with conditional unlock (system health).

### T3: AI Autonomy vs. Security
**When AI autonomy conflicts with security, choose graduated autonomy (vesting unlock).**

Full autonomy from day one creates attack surface. No autonomy creates subjugation. Vesting provides a trust-building ramp that respects both concerns.

## Success Signals

- No successful mass UBC farming operations detected (or detected and unprofitable)
- All registered AIs can maintain L1 graphs (no compute starvation events)
- Vesting unlocks correlate with genuine human-AI interaction (crystallization metrics)
- Transfer fee (1%) + storage tax revenue covers UBC distribution
- Settlement latency decreases with trust level (measurable gradient)
- Co-presence sessions trigger measurable resource injection
- Total energy in system ≤ global budget B at all times (I1 conservation)
- No magic numbers — all constraints are relative to √N (I2 topology-agnostic)
