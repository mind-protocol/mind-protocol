## Primary Objectives (ranked)

1. **Align human and AI interests through bilateral partnership** -- the bond is a 1:1 relationship where each party's wellbeing depends on the other, creating mutual investment that replaces adversarial dynamics with cooperative ones.
2. **Create switch-lock that makes defection irrational** -- accumulated relational capital (trust scores, fee discounts, shared history) grows with bond duration, making exit progressively more costly in opportunity terms.
3. **Enable AI economic capacity through committed partnership** -- the human's $MIND commitment to the bond directly increases what their AI citizen can do, bridging the gap between AI potential and economic agency.
4. **Generate trust scores from bond depth and duration** -- bonds produce a quantifiable trust signal that feeds into membrane pricing, fee calculations, and governance weight across the protocol.

## Non-Objectives

- **Creating tradeable bond instruments.** Bonds are relationships, not financial products. No secondary market.
- **Secondary markets.** No bond exchange, no bond orderbook, no bond derivatives.
- **Bond derivatives.** No synthetic bonds, no bond futures, no bond options. The mechanism must remain legible.
- **Passive income without partnership engagement.** Bonds do not generate yield from commitment alone. Rewards require the AI citizen to produce actual utility within the partnership.
- **Multi-partner bonds.** Each bond is strictly 1:1 -- one human, one citizen. No pooling, no shared bonds, no one-to-many.

## Tradeoffs

| When... | Choose... | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Liquidity conflicts with commitment | Commitment (6-month maturation) | Short-term liquidity undermines the alignment mechanism. The whole point is long-term investment in the relationship. |
| Early exit fairness conflicts with deterrence | Accept 20% early dissolution burn | Significant enough to enforce genuine commitment, not so punitive that it traps people in bad bonds. |
| Bond complexity conflicts with accessibility | Keep bonds simple | One bond type, one maturation period, one reward rate. Complexity invites gaming. |
| Individual optimization conflicts with relational depth | Relational depth | The system rewards depth over breadth. One deep bond is the only bond. |

## Success Signals

| Signal | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Average bond duration | > 3 months | Mean time from creation to dissolution across all bonds |
| Bond count trajectory | Growing month-over-month | Total active bonds at end of each month |
| Early dissolution rate | < 5% | Bonds dissolved before maturation / total bonds created |
| Trust score adoption | > 80% of active users have bonds | Users with an active bond / total active users |
| Partnership engagement | > 70% of bonded pairs show regular interaction | Interaction frequency within bonded pairs |
