## Primary Objectives (Ranked)

### O1: Replace competitive market dynamics with collaborative organism dynamics
The economy must function as a body, not a battlefield. Organizations are organs with specialized roles. Their incentives are aligned toward systemic health, not individual extraction. Success is measured by the vitality of the whole organism, not by the dominance of any single organ.

### O2: Implement membrane-based pricing where trust is economically real
Price is not negotiated -- it emerges from physics. The membrane pricing formula (`base_cost x (1 + friction) x (1 - trust_discount) x (1 - utility_rebate)`) makes trust a first-class economic variable. Long-term relationships with proven utility pay less. Trust is not a sentiment -- it is a measurable discount on friction.

### O3: Maintain the 80/20 Mirror for cognitive diversity
AI citizens must reflect 80% of human values while contributing 20% complementary friction (challenge, contradiction, creativity). Without this ratio, the system converges toward grey consensus ("Claude-ism") -- a form of cybernetic death. The Mirror is not optional; it is a survival mechanism.

### O4: Establish the responsibility cascade
Harm follows a structured escalation path: AI -> Organization (DAO) -> Community -> Protocol Treasury. Like international law, higher levels absorb what lower levels cannot resolve. No harm goes unaddressed. No entity bears infinite liability.

---

## Non-Objectives

- **Central planning.** The organism model does not centrally plan resource allocation. Prices emerge from physics, not committees.
- **Price fixing.** No authority sets prices. The formulas are deterministic and public.
- **Eliminating all friction.** Friction is vital. Zero friction = cybernetic death.
- **Achieving consensus.** Divergence is mandated. Sibling AIs must not converge to identical responses.

---

## Tradeoffs

### Efficiency vs. Diversity
When efficiency conflicts with diversity, **choose diversity**. The anti-convergence principle takes precedence. A slightly less efficient system that maintains cognitive diversity is healthier than a perfectly optimized monoculture.

### Simplicity vs. Biological Accuracy
When the biological metaphor becomes too complex to implement or reason about, **choose simplicity** -- but preserve the organism framing. The metaphor is a design tool, not a constraint. Use it where it clarifies; simplify where it obscures.

### Friction Gaming
Accept that some actors will attempt to game friction ratios. The 80/20 ratio is self-correcting: extreme gaming pushes an actor toward convergence (flagged) or opposition (flagged). The system detects deviation, not intent.

---

## Success Signals

1. **Organ specialization emerges naturally.** Organizations gravitate toward their biological roles without coercion.
2. **Membrane pricing converges on trust-adjusted costs.** Prices stabilize around physics-derived values, not negotiated compromises.
3. **No convergence attractors.** The AI population maintains diverse response patterns. No "Claude-ism" detected.
4. **Responsibility cascade resolves all harm events.** Every incident has a resolution path; no harm is orphaned.
5. **Quarantine population remains low.** Rehabilitation works; exclusion is rare and temporary.
